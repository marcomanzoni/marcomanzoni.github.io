## SnowDrone
**Start**: November 2026  
**Role**: WP Leader  
**Funding agency**: European Space Agency (ESA)  
**Description of the project**: SnowDrone is a project proposed to ESA to study snow using drone-mounted SAR systems. It includes C- and X-band measurement campaigns combined with in situ observations. The data will support Hydroterra+ snow products and the development of new retrieval methods.

## Sentinel-1D Interferometric In-Orbit Commissioning Phase

**Start**: October 2025    
**Role**: Principal Investigator  
**Funding agency**: European Space Agency (ESA)  
**Description of the project**: Contribution to the interferometric commissioning of ESA’s Sentinel-1D satellite, including ionospheric phase correction, orbit error estimation, burst synchronization verification and permanent-scatterer-based calibration.

## HydroDrone

**Start**: September 2024  
**Role**: WP Leader
**Funding agency**: European Space Agency (ESA)  
**Description of the project**: HydroDrone is an ESA-funded campaign using drone-mounted C-band SAR to investigate short-term interferometric temporal decorrelation over vegetated areas. The project supports the Hydroterra+ mission and the development of methods for soil moisture retrieval.

## BIOMASS Interferometric In-Orbit Commissioning Phase

**Start**: November 2023  
**Role**: Sub-contractor
**Funding agency**: Aresys s.r.l.  
**Description of the project**: Contribution to the interferometric commissioning of ESA’s BIOMASS mission, supporting the preparation and analysis of P-band SAR data and the assessment of interferometric performance.
